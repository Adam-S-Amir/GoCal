Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026
Plain English Summary: GoCal and its AI companion Callie only access your Google Calendar to perform the direct actions you ask for (like checking your schedule, moving a class, or adding a study session). We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers, and you can disconnect at any time.

1. Introduction

GoCal ("we", "our", or "the application") is a scheduling assistant application featuring Callie, designed to help students and busy professionals manage their agendas through conversational commands.

2. Google User Data We Access

When you choose to connect your Google Account with GoCal, we request access to the Google Calendar API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar). We access:

  • Calendar Events: Event titles, start and end times, and locations so Callie can read your schedule and check for open time slots.
  • Basic Profile Info: Your email address and display name to identify your authenticated session.

3. How We Use Your Calendar Data

Your calendar data is used strictly to deliver user-initiated scheduling functionality:

  • Displaying your upcoming daily and weekly agenda when you ask "what's my schedule?".
  • Creating, rescheduling, or removing events when you explicitly command Callie to do so (e.g., "move Spanish to 8pm").
  • Analyzing schedule gaps to recommend free time windows for socializing or downtime.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your Google Calendar data to any third parties.

Your Google OAuth access tokens are stored in secure, encrypted, HTTP-only session cookies and are transmitted over HTTPS. We do not use your personal calendar data to train public AI models.

5. Revoking Access and Data Deletion

You have full control over your Google Account permissions. You can disconnect GoCal at any time by:

  • Clicking "Sign Out" or "Disconnect" inside GoCal.
  • Visiting your Google Account Security & Permissions page and revoking access for GoCal.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via GitHub or through the developer contact info listed in the application.
